## Important Architecture Notes
- All screens use `CompositionLocalProvider(LocalLayoutDirection provides LayoutDirection.Rtl)`
- `ModalBottomSheet` requires `@OptIn(ExperimentalMaterial3Api::class)` on the composable
- `HorizontalPager` requires `@OptIn(ExperimentalFoundationApi::class)`
- Draft system: `weekStart = "__TEMP_DRAFT__"` in Room DB
- `isEditingExistingSchedule = true` means viewing from History (smart save, no draft involvement)
